tags: [process]
---
Declare a severity, open a channel, name an incident commander. A
[SEV1](/glossary/sev1.md) pages the owning team through the
[pager rotation](/glossary/pager-rotation.md) and freezes deploys.

Every incident gets a postmortem in `incidents/` within five working days.
